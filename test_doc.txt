Vostro World is a premium fitness and wellness center located in Rawalpindi, Pakistan.
It offers state of the art gym equipment, personal training, yoga classes, and cafe services.
The cafe serves healthy meals, protein shakes, smoothies, and energy drinks.
Membership plans include monthly, quarterly, and annual packages.
Monthly membership costs 5000 PKR, quarterly is 12000 PKR, and annual is 40000 PKR.
Personal training sessions are available at 2000 PKR per session.
The gym is open from 6am to 11pm every day including weekends.
Cafe items include grilled chicken, protein bars, water, Gatorade, and fresh juices.
